# Eighths against quarters

The hardest drill in this set. One hand plays **eighth notes**, two for every quarter note in the other hand.
In bar 9 the hands swap, so the left hand plays the eighths.

```staff
right: C4/1!:0.5 D4/2:0.5 E4/3!:0.5 D4/2:0.5 C4/1!:0.5 D4/2:0.5 E4/3!:0.5 D4/2:0.5
left: C3/5! E3/3! G3/1! E3/3!
caption: Each quarter note in the left hand goes with two eighths in the right. Blue notes are played together.
```

## Pay attention to
- Count "1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and". The quarter-note hand plays only on the **numbers**.
- Eighths must be **even**. Play them softly, and don't rush when the other hand comes in.
- Start with timed mode at **50 %** and speed up only when no notes turn orange.

## Learn
Steady eighths over a slower part in the other hand are in almost every piece. When this drill feels easy,
you are ready for simple pieces with both hands.
