# Strong and weak fingers

You play neighbouring fingers in pairs: 1-2, 2-3, 3-4, 4-5. The right hand plays first, then the left hand.

```staff
right: C4/1 D4/2 C4/1 D4/2 F4/4! G4/5! F4/4! G4/5!
caption: Fingers 4 and 5 (blue) are the weakest: make them sound as strong as 1 and 2.
```

## Pay attention to
- Keep your fingers **curved**, as if holding a ball, and play with the fingertip.
- Only the playing finger moves. Keep the **wrist still** and the other fingers resting on their keys.
- In the left hand, finger 1 is on G, so the same pairs go **down** the keyboard:

```staff
left: G3/1 F3/2 G3/1 F3/2 D3/4! C3/5! D3/4! C3/5!
caption: Left hand, 1-2 and then 4-5 (blue).
```

## Learn
Evenness is the goal: all notes the same volume and the same length. Timed mode at a slow speed helps.
