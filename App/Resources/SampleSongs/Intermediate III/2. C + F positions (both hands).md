# The left hand moves further

In F position the right thumb goes to F4, and the left hand moves down to **F2** (left 5 = F2).
The left hand jumps further than the right, so start the move early.

```staff
right: C4/1:4 F4/1! G4/2 A4/3 G4/2
left: C3/5:4 r:4 F2/5! G2/4 A2/3 G2/4
caption: After the whole notes, both hands move to F (blue). The left hand goes from C3 down to F2.
```

## Pay attention to
- **F2** is in the space **just below the bass staff**. The bass clef's two dots sit around the F3 line, one octave higher.
- Look ahead during the whole notes in bar 8: both hands move at the same time.
- The left hand doesn't use finger **2** here (its key would be B♭).

## Learn
The same finger pattern sounds different in a new position: you're playing the same shapes in **another key**.
