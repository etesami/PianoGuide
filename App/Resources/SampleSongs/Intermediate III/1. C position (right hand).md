# One finger for each key

In **C position** the right thumb (1) sits on middle C, and each finger has its own key:
1 = C, 2 = D, 3 = E, 4 = F, 5 = G.

```staff
right: C4/1 D4/2 E4/3 F4/4 G4/5 F4/4 E4/3 D4/2 C4/1! E4/3! G4/5! E4/3 C4/1:4
caption: The first 4 bars. Each step up on the staff is the next finger. Blue: C, E, G, the C chord.
```

## Pay attention to
- In the practice the finger number is shown only on the **first note of each bar**. Read it, then follow the notes.
- Keep all five fingers resting **over their keys**, even the ones not playing. The hand doesn't move.
- **Whole notes** (hollow, no stem) last **4 beats**. Count "1 2 3 4" while you hold them.

## Learn
C, E and G with fingers 1, 3, 5 are the **C major chord**, played one note at a time.
