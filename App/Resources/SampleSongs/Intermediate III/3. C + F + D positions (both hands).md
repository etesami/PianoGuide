# F# in both hands

In D position the left hand sits on D3 to A3 (left 5 = D3), and **finger 3 plays F#** in both hands.

```staff
right: D4/1 E4/2 F#4/3! E4/2
left: r:4 D3/5 E3/4 F#3/3! E3/4
caption: Watch for the sharp (♯) on both staves: finger 3 plays F# (blue) in each hand.
```

## Pay attention to
- Both hands play **F#**, not F, while in D position.
- Three position changes: C → F → D → C. Each time, use the whole notes at the end of a phrase to move both hands.
- Keep the hands **relaxed** on the black key: move the hand forward, don't reach with a straight finger.

## Learn
The C, F and D positions start on different notes but have the **same shape**: practising them trains your hand to move, not just your fingers.
