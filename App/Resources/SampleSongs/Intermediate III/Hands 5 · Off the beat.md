# Off the beat

Until now the hands always started together. Here one hand comes in **one beat later**, so a note
starts in one hand **while the other hand is holding**. In bar 9 the hands swap.

```staff
right: C4/1:2 E4/3:2 G4/5:2 F4/4:2
left: r C3/5!:2 E3/3 r G3/1!:2 F3/2
caption: The left hand rests on beat 1 and comes in on beat 2 (blue), in the middle of the right hand's half note.
```

## Pay attention to
- Count "1 2 3 4" out loud. The left hand starts on **2**, not on 1.
- Keep holding the half note in one hand while the other hand plays. Both notes should sound at once.
- In bars 5–8 the right hand plays quarters, so the hands meet on beats 2 and 3:

```staff
right: C4/1 D4/2 E4/3 F4/4
left: r C3/5!:2 E3/3
caption: Left hand comes in on beat 2 while the right hand keeps playing quarters.
```

## Learn
Hands that start at different times need each hand to keep its own count. Try timed mode at a slow speed for this one.
