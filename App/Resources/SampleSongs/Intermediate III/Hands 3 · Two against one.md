# Two against one

Both hands move now, at different speeds. One hand plays **quarter notes** and the other plays **half notes**,
so the hands land together on every other beat. In bar 9 the hands swap.

```staff
right: C4/1! D4/2 E4/3! F4/4 G4/5! F4/4 E4/3! D4/2
left: C3/5!:2 E3/3!:2 G3/1!:2 E3/3!:2
caption: Blue notes are played together, on beats 1 and 3. On beats 2 and 4 only the right hand plays.
```

## Pay attention to
- Count "1 2 3 4": the half notes start on **1 and 3**. Say "together" on those beats if it helps.
- Each half note must **last two beats**. Don't let go when the quarter-note hand plays its second note.
- After the swap the left hand plays the quarters. Go slower for those 8 bars if you need to.

## Learn
Two notes in one hand against one in the other is the most common pattern in simple pieces with an accompaniment.
