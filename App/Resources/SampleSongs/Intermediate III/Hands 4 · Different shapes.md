# Different shapes

Both hands play **quarter notes together** on every beat, but each hand has its **own shape**. The left hand
repeats a short pattern (C E G E) while the right hand plays a melody. In bar 9 the right hand takes the pattern.

```staff
right: C4/1 D4/2 E4/3 C4/1 G4/5 F4/4 E4/3 D4/2
left: C3/5! E3/3! G3/1! E3/3! C3/5! E3/3! G3/1! E3/3!
caption: The left-hand pattern (blue) is the same in both bars. The right-hand melody goes up, then down.
```

## Pay attention to
- First play the **pattern alone** a few times until your hand knows it without looking.
- Then add the melody. Read the **right hand** on the staff; let the pattern hand play from memory.
- Sometimes the hands move the same way, sometimes opposite ways. Go slowly where they move apart.

## Learn
This is how much piano music works: one hand repeats an accompaniment pattern while the other plays the tune.
