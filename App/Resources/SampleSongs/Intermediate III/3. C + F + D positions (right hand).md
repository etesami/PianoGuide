# Your first black key: F#

In **D position** the thumb is on D: 1 = D, 2 = E, **3 = F#**, 4 = G, 5 = A.
The route is C → F → D → back to C.

```staff
right: D4/1 E4/2 F#4/3! G4/4 A4/5 G4/4 F#4/3! E4/2
caption: F# (blue) has a sharp sign (♯) in front of it: play the black key with finger 3.
```

## Pay attention to
- **F#** is the black key just right of F.
- Move your hand **a little forward** (toward the piano) so finger 3 reaches the black key without twisting.
- From F position to D position the hand moves **down**:

```staff
right: F4/1:4 D4/1! E4/2 F#4/3 G4/4
caption: Hold F, then move the thumb down to D (blue).
```

## Learn
A sharp (♯) means "play the next key to the right", usually a black key.
