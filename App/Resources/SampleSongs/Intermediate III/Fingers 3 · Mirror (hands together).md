# Same fingers in both hands

Both hands play together with the **same finger numbers**. Because the hands are mirror images,
they move in **opposite directions**.

```staff
right: C4/1! D4/2 E4/3 F4/4 G4/5 F4/4 E4/3 D4/2
left: G3/1! F3/2 E3/3 D3/4 C3/5 D3/4 E3/3 F3/2
caption: Same fingers, opposite directions: the right hand goes up while the left goes down. Blue: both thumbs.
```

## Pay attention to
- Think in **finger numbers**, not note names: "1 2 3 4" in both hands at once.
- Both thumbs are near the middle (right 1 = C4, left 1 = G3), and the little fingers are on the outside.
- Play both notes **exactly together**. If one hand is late, slow down.

## Learn
Mirror movement feels natural because both hands do the same thing. That's why it comes before the next drill.
