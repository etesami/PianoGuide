# Taking turns

The first step to playing with both hands: **the hands never play at the same time**. They take turns, and
the turns get shorter: first a whole bar each, then half a bar, then a single beat. Only the last note is together.

```staff
right: C4/1 D4/2 r:2 E4/3 F4/4 r:2
left: r:2 C3/5! D3/4! r:2 E3/3! F3/2!
caption: Half-bar turns: the left hand (blue) echoes the right hand's notes an octave lower.
```

## Pay attention to
- While one hand plays, the other **rests on its keys**, ready. Don't lift it away.
- Keep a **steady beat** through the hand change: there should be no gap when the other hand takes over.
- Count out loud, "1 2 3 4". From bar 9, the right hand plays on 1 and 3, the left on 2 and 4:

```staff
right: C4/1 r D4/2 r E4/3 r F4/4 r
left: r C3/5! r D3/4! r E3/3! r F3/2!
caption: One beat each. Together the hands make an even line of quarter notes.
```

## Learn
Your brain switches attention from one hand to the other. Getting that switch smooth comes before playing both at once.
