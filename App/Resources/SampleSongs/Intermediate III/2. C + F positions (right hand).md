# Moving to a new position

After 8 bars in C position, the hand **moves up** so the thumb sits on **F**. At the end it comes back to C.

```staff
right: C4/1:4 F4/1! G4/2 A4/3 G4/2
caption: Hold the whole note C, then move the whole hand: the thumb lands on F (blue).
```

## Pay attention to
- The move happens after the **whole note** in bar 8. While you hold it, look ahead at the next bar's finger number.
- **Lift the whole hand** and move it as one shape. Don't stretch your thumb over to F.
- The hand keeps its shape, only the place changes: the same finger numbers now play different notes.

```staff
right: F4/1 G4/2 A4/3 C5/5:2!
caption: F position: 1 = F, 2 = G, 3 = A, 5 = C (blue). Finger 4 would be B♭, a black key, so it isn't used yet.
```
