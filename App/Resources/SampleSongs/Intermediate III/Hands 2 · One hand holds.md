# One hand holds

Now the hands play together, but only one of them moves. One hand plays a **whole note and holds it**
for four beats while the other plays four quarter notes. In bar 9 the hands swap jobs.

```staff
right: C4/1! D4/2 E4/3 F4/4 G4/5! F4/4 E4/3 D4/2
left: C3/5!:4 G3/1!:4
caption: Press the blue notes together, then keep the left hand down while the right hand moves.
```

## Pay attention to
- Play the first note of each bar with **both hands exactly together**.
- Keep the holding hand **pressed down** for all four beats. Don't let it come up when the other hand moves.
- Bars 9–16 are the same music with the hands swapped. This half is usually harder:

```staff
right: C4/1!:4 G4/5!:4
left: C3/5! D3/4 E3/3 F3/2 G3/1! F3/2 E3/3 D3/4
caption: Now the right hand holds and the left hand plays the quarters.
```

## Learn
Holding one hand still while the other moves is the start of hand independence.
