# Skips on the staff

Now each pair skips a finger: 1-3, 2-4, 3-5. Skipping a finger means skipping a key.

```staff
right: C4/1 E4/3 C4/1 E4/3 D4/2! F4/4! D4/2! F4/4! E4/3 G4/5 E4/3 G4/5
caption: C-E and E-G go line to line; D-F (blue) goes space to space. Every skip is one line or space apart.
```

## Pay attention to
- Learn to **see** the skip shape on the staff: line to line, or space to space, is always one finger apart.
- The finger in the middle stays **down over its key** but doesn't play.
- **2-4** (blue) is the hardest pair: finger 4 is weak, so keep it curved and play it firmly.

## Learn
The distance between two notes is an **interval**. A skip like C to E is a **third**; fingers 1-3 cover it in C position.
