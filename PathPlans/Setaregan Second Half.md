The rest of Ghoghaye Setaregan after the verse: the chorus, the climb, the high part and the ending (score bars 27–116), learned the same way as "Two Hands Together": each part alone, then the hands meet a little at a time, then the parts are joined. The last lesson is the whole song. Best after finishing "Two Hands Together". Short sessions work best: 15–20 minutes, then stop.

## Lesson: Meet the second half
goal: Hear where the song goes after the verse before learning it, part by part.
tip: Listen for the chorus (repeated notes, from bar 27), the climb up to C, the high part (from bar 79, played twice), and the verse tune coming back at the end.
tip: Count 1-2-3 with the music: the left hand keeps its waltz almost all the way.

## Lesson: The chorus
goal: Bars 27–34: three quick repeated notes after a rest, stepping down C, B, A, while the left hand starts every bar alone.
tip: The right hand starts on beat 2: the left hand's low note on 1 is your signal.
tip: Move the hand up one key (F position: thumb on F, 5 on C) for bars 27–32, then back to thumb on E.
tip: Repeated notes: let the key come up a little between them; the same finger plays all three.
tip: Bars 29 and 33 end with a short turn, A-G and G-F: the second note is very short, almost a grace note.
tip: Left hand: a new chord in bar 1, A and C over the low E (5, 2, 1), as in the verse.

## Lesson: The high part
goal: Bars 79–92: the highest part of the song, up to E with finger 5. The song plays it twice, so learning it once covers 28 bars.
tip: Bars 79–82: A position, thumb on A, so C is 3 and the high E is 5.
tip: Bars 83–86: one key lower, G position: B is 3.
tip: From bar 87 the thumb is back on E, and the end is much like "Coming down": A G, F G F, E.
tip: Left hand: A minor for the first four bars, then E minor and F.
