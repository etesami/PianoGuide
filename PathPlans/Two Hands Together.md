# Two Hands Together
id: two-hands-setaregan

time: 3/4

For when each hand plays fine alone but they fall apart together. Ten short lessons on the verse of Ghoghaye Setaregan, a waltz in 3/4: first the left hand learns its pattern until it plays by itself, then the hands meet a little at a time. Each step opens when the one before is passed. Short sessions work best: 15–20 minutes, then stop, even in the middle of a lesson.

## Parts
Section 1: 3-10
Section 2: 11-18
Section 3: 19-26
Verse: 3-26

## Lesson: Meet the song
goal: Know what each hand plays in the first 8 bars before putting them together.
tip: The song is a waltz: count 1-2-3, 1-2-3, with the weight on 1.
tip: The left hand plays a low note on 1 and a two-note chord on 2 and 3: "oom-pah-pah".
tip: Right-hand thumb on E: E 1, F 2, G 3, A 4.

## Lesson: Left hand on autopilot
goal: The left-hand pattern plays itself, so all your attention is free for the right hand. This is the most important lesson: take your time here.
tip: Count out loud while you play: "ONE two three".
tip: Once the notes are known, look at the screen, not at your hand.
tip: In timed mode, don't stop after a mistake: keep going and catch the next 1.
